Starting up…

🎒 How students log in

  • No password needed. Tap I'm a Student and type the class code your teacher gives you (for example SMITH482).
  • Find your name on the class list and tap it.
  • Pick your avatar the first time — you can change it any time from the menu.
  • Math Facts Levels: answer quickly and correctly to master every fact and unlock the next level.
  • Sky Climber (×2 to ×10): every climb is 20 questions. Answer in under 3 seconds to climb a full floor and clear the fact, or under 6 seconds to climb half a floor.

🧑‍🏫 How teachers get started

  • Create your teacher account with an email and password, and choose your class grade level.
  • You get a unique class code made from your last name plus numbers (e.g. SMITH482). Share it with your class.
  • Add each student by name in your dashboard. They log in with the class code — no student emails or passwords required.
  • Move a student to another teacher with that teacher's class code, or remove a student from your roster.
  • Track progress for both Math Facts Levels and the ×2–×10 Sky Climber modules, and reset levels when needed.
